# Nexoryx: The Cinematic AI Director

## Project Overview
Nexoryx is a state-of-the-art cinematic AI directing and editing platform designed for modern content creators. It shifts the paradigm from traditional manual video editing to a high-level "Directorial" experience, where AI assists in the creative process of sequence building, timing, and visual refinement.

## The Core Concept
Nexoryx allows users to transform a single theme or topic into a structured cinematic production. By leveraging a dual-panel interface (Storyboard + Timeline), it bridges the gap between conceptual storytelling and technical execution.

## Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Cinematic Storyboard Editor
*   **Scene-Based Workflow**: Organize your vision into distinct, manageable scenes with unique descriptions and camera motions.
*   **Integrated Management**: Seamlessly add, delete, and reorder scenes to perfect the narrative flow.
*   **Direct-to-Scene Cropping**: Intelligent aspect ratio selection (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5) optimized for different social distribution channels.

### 2. High-Performance Architect Canvas
*   **Real-Time Synchronization**: A fully reactive canvas where video playback, timecodes, and the interactive timeline are perfectly in sync.
*   **Dynamic Metadata Integration**: Automatic duration detection that adjusts scene lengths based on actual video media (e.g., your primary 15-second reveal).
*   **Visual Feedback Loop**: Intuitive controls for playback, fullscreen preview, and frame-accurate seeking.

### 3. Professional Interactive Timeline
*   **Precision Ruler System**: A professional-grade ruler with 1-second interval ticks and 5-second timecode labels for precise navigation.
*   **Multi-Track Architecture**: Visual and audio tracks that clearly delineate the different layers of the cinematic production.
*   **Coordinate-Based Seeking**: Navigate through your project instantly by interacting directly with any part of the tracks.

### 4. AI Director Chat (Integrated Agent)
*   **Creative Assistance**: A dedicated AI agent acting as your "Assistant Director" to help refine prompts, suggest lighting changes, or optimize scene transitions.
*   **Quick Actions**: One-click shortcuts for complex cinematic adjustments like "Slow Motion" or "Color Grading."

### 5. Seamless Export & Persistence
*   **Instant Saving**: Integrated save functionality with real-time visual feedback to ensure work is never lost.
*   **Direct Export Path**: A streamlined transition from editing to the final rendering and sharing phase.

## Technical Foundation
*   **Frontend Architecture**: Built with React and Vite for lightning-fast development and runtime performance.
*   **Motion & Interactions**: Powered by Framer Motion for premium, smooth-as-silk UI transitions and micro-animations.
*   **Visual Language**: A bespoke dark-mode aesthetic utilizing an HSL-tailored color palette (Vivid Indigo, Deep Violet, and Matte Slate) for a premium, professional feel.
*   **Scalability**: Component-based design allowing for easy integration of future AI rendering pipelines.

## The Vision
Nexoryx is built for the era of AI-native creation—where the barrier between an idea and a cinematic masterpiece is reduced to a simple conversation with an AI Director.
